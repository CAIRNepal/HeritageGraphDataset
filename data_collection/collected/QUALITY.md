## Quality checks (column `quality_flags`)

| flag | meaning | rows |
|---|---|---:|
| `no_district` | inside the province but in no district boundary (boundary gap); kept in `_no_district/` | 0 |
| `no_name` | OSM feature without any name | 2,873 |
| `unusual_religion` | OSM `religion` value outside the usual list | 16 |
| `bahai_probably_bahi_monastery` | `religion=bahai` on a feature named *bahā/bahī* (Newar monastery): likely a tagging error | 4 |
| `possible_duplicate` | same name as another OSM feature within 30 m (e.g. a node and a building outline) | 345 |
| `no_english_label` | Wikidata item without an English label | 31 |
| `no_class` | Wikidata item kept only for its heritage designation | 0 |
| `danam_district_differs` | DANAM's own district field differs from the district boundary that contains the point | 0 |
| `no_coordinates_district_from_p131` | Wikidata item without coordinates, placed by the one district in its P131 chain | 74 |
| `no_coordinates_no_district` | Wikidata item without coordinates whose P131 chain names no single district; in `_no_coordinates/wikidata.csv` | 37 |
| `settlement_not_a_structure` | Wikidata item is a town or municipality (kept for its heritage designation) | 5 |

Other columns that help judge quality: `heritage_tagged` (OSM `historic=*` or `heritage=*`), `category`, `recent_looking` and `recent_signals` (no heritage tag and religion Christian, a modern-sounding name, or a start_date from 1950: a heuristic), `duplicate_group`.

**Coverage caveat.** Most OSM features are current places of worship without a heritage tag; only the `historic` category is heritage by tagging. Their age cannot be read from OSM.
