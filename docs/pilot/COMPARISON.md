## Recent-looking OSM features

Heuristic, applied only to features without a `historic=*` or `heritage=*` tag: religion Christian, a name with words such as church, fellowship, satsang, ashram, school, hall or bhawan, or a `start_date` from 1950 on. Features without these signals are not necessarily old: most OSM places of worship carry no date.

**Lalitpur**: 46 of 604 OSM features (signals: religion 37, name 38). Examples: St. Marys School Church (node/1892823655; religion=christian, name: school); Everest Sharansthan Babtist Church (node/1937660001; religion=christian, name: church); Love Church and Love library (node/1937660002; religion=christian, name: church); Holy Christian Church (node/1937710527; religion=christian, name: church); Nepal Naya Darsan Mandali (node/2684769395; religion=christian).

**Chitwan**: 16 of 265 OSM features (signals: name 9, religion 14). Examples: Trinity Church (node/3392281231; name: church); Bharatpur Baptist chruch (node/3392360967; religion=christian); Nawa Jiwan AG Chruch (node/3392360974; religion=christian); Bharatpur Christian Fellowship (node/3415398777; religion=christian, name: fellowship); Chitwan church (node/5549468721; name: church).

**Kanchanpur**: 3 of 100 OSM features (signals: name 3). Examples: Radha Soami Satsang Beas Nepal (node/9530825960; name: satsang); Shree Bandebi Niranjandev Aashram (node/9530825964; name: ashram); Jagdaguru Ashram Mahakali Shardapeeth (node/9801965342; name: ashram).

## Notes

- **DANAM covers neither Chitwan nor Kanchanpur.** None of its monuments has coordinates in either district or names either as its district; its westernmost records (33) are in the neighbouring hill districts Dadeldhura (Ajaymeru, Amargadhi) and Baitadi (Dasharathchand). In these two districts only OSM and Wikidata can be compared.
- **Wikidata is sparse everywhere:** 11 items in Lalitpur, 5 in Chitwan, none in Kanchanpur for the classes queried (temple, stupa, monument, heritage site, or any heritage designation).
- **OSM is mostly current places of worship:** outside Lalitpur, fewer than 5 % of OSM features carry a `historic=*` tag.
- OSM tagging errors seen: in Lalitpur, two Newar monasteries named *bahī* are tagged `religion=bahai`.
